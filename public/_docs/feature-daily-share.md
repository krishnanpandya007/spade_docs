# **Daily Share**
--------------------------


# [*Motive*](#motive) 
----------------------------------------------
Create more interaction between users/community. take them to open about their voice, by making the daily interactions audified/textified gives more freedom to do record/write  your day without wearing unnessesary makeups 😅



# [*Concept*](#concept) 
--------
Share your daily life movements via audio/text on this platform. Seek whats going on with other people's day by exploring such Daily Share(s)

This can be in any format (audio/text)


# [*Steps (Internally)*](#steps) 
-----------
* Must be Logged In
* Click _How's Today_ option floating on your home page 
* Stack up your daily Share information and **Make it fly**
